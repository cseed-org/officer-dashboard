## Allowed use

You may refer to CSEED by name factually, for example to identify the upstream
project, and share unmodified links to the official project or [CSEED website](https://cseed.co).
Keep required copyright and attribution notices when redistributing the code.

## Forks and deployments

Before deploying a fork, replace the covered brand assets with your own artwork
and update the displayed project name and branding. Do not use the CSEED logo
in forks or deployments without written permission. Do not imply that CSEED
endorses, operates or sponsors your version, or use confusingly similar branding.
The presence of these assets in this repository does not grant permission to reuse them.

## Permission requests

Contact CSEED through the contact channels on [cseed.co](https://cseed.co).
Describe the assets, intended use, audience and duration. Obtain written permission
before using reserved branding; silence is not permission.
