Option A · Studio board

A calm, Linear-style board with Trello-size drop lanes. Four full-height columns (To do / Doing / Review / Done), each with a count and a thin progress bar. Rich cards: type icon in its colour, event and date, a due chip that turns amber and red, assignee avatar, requester, and the two design-file buttons. Quick filters are chips with counts; type filters are icon chips. Clicking a card opens a peek panel over the right of the board (deep-linkable), so you keep your place. While dragging, allowed columns get a dashed outline, and columns you can't drop into fade with the reason. On phones, a 4-tab stage switcher shows one column at a time, and the detail opens as a bottom sheet with a big “Move to …” button. This is the most familiar option and the cheapest to build.
