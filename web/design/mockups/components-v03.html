Motion rules

Onboarding motion rules
MomentMotionReduced motion
Step forwardOld step fades and moves 16px left (240ms). New step’s headline, lede and form rise 8px and slide in from 16px right, staggered 120ms (420ms each, ease-soft).Instant swap. Focus moves to the step heading.
Step backMirror of forward: content moves right.Instant swap. Focus moves to the step heading.
ProgressThe current segment fills left to right (320ms).Segment is filled on arrival. “Step 2 of 6” is in the list’s accessible name.
Polaroids (step 1, done)Settle from 40px below at 0° to their tilt, 900ms, staggered 120ms after a 200ms wait. Hover lifts 4px.Shown at rest at their tilt. No hover lift.
HighlighterStatic. It never draws or animates.Same.
Done stepChecklist items rise in sequence; nothing loops, no confetti.All items shown at once.
